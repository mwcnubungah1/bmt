# BMT Version

Current release: `v1.1.0`

`v1.0.0` baseline is preserved by the migration history. `v1.0.1` contains the schema-cache and generated-type synchronization fix. `v1.0.2` adds atomic teller deposits with balanced journals and transaction idempotency. `v1.0.3` adds daily teller cash-session opening and closing controls. `v1.0.4` adds responsive account search, safe submission, pending transfer deposits, and printable receipts. `v1.0.5` adds real-browser teller E2E coverage against local Supabase, a UI cash-session gate, live teller cash balance, and RPC result-type corrections. `v1.0.6` repairs savings-account creation on completed onboarding, separates customer loan-option loading, and verifies Agil's account and product options in a local browser test. `v1.0.7` adds versioned murabahah products, server-side simulation, requirements, snapshot-based loan submissions, and the customer simulation UI.

`v1.1.0` locks the savings-product monthly deposit and dormant-account rules, plus the customer loan-document upload flow and production deployment preparation.
